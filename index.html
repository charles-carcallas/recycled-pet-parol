DIY Build Sequence (~88 Cut PET Parts)

▾
Materials: ~80× cut green PET bottle tops/bodies, ~27× 5-lobed bottle bottoms, ~8× clear PET panels, 1× 1.50m bamboo pole (3.4cm Ø), #18 galvanized tie wire & warm LEDs.
  1. 011.5m Bamboo Spine: Lash horizontal & diagonal brace sticks onto the 1.50m pole using #18 tie wire.
  2. 02Inner Star & 24 Cut Bottles: Wire an 8-point inner star inside the 0.55m ring and fill each bay with 3 radial cut bottles.
  3. 03Outer 16-Pt Star & LEDs: Mount 16 faceted 3D pyramidal points around the ring circumference with dim warm LEDs inside.
  4. 04Cut-Bottle Scalloped Halo: Wire 8 fan arches of 7 fanned 3D cut plastic bottles each around the outer star; cap the seams.
  5. 05Lobed-Bottom Center & Tails: Fill the inner star center with 27× 5-lobed bottoms; hang curled chains & bottle bells down to -0.74m.
